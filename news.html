09

July 24, 2026

EMDP team wins the Grand Prize at DGIST AI Build Week

EMDP Lab participated together in DGIST AI Build Week, where students Junhyeong Seo, In Ja Park, and Jihyun Lee competed as the EMDP team and won the Grand Prize (1st place).

DGIST AI Build Week에 랩실이 다같이 참가하여 서준형/박인자/이지현 학생 EMDP 팀이 1등을 했습니다! 정말 축하드립니다.

Negasi and Jihyun Yu's team also took on the challenge. Although they did not receive an award this time, let's cheer them on to try again next time!

아쉽게 수상하지 못한 Negasi, 유지현 학생팀도 다음에 한번더 도전해 봅시다!

08

July 6–7, 2026

Students attend the Advanced Materials Academy in Seoul

On July 6 and 7, EMDP Lab students attended the Advanced Materials Academy in Seoul, hosted by the Polymer Society of Korea. It was a valuable opportunity to build advanced knowledge of polymer composites.

7월 6일, 7일 양일간 서울에서 한국고분자학회가 주최하는 첨단소재 아카데미에 학생들이 참가하였습니다. 고분자 복합소재 관련 첨단 지식을 쌓을 수 있는 좋은 기회였습니다.
